Meet Rosebud, the Wagging Dog

2026-09-06

She is the namesake of Wagging Dog Farm. And she is a very, very good girl.

Rosie has been with us since 2013, along for the wild ride of this life. She’s a quiet and loving presence. She watches you. She cheers you on, with her loving eyes. Not much of an athlete, she’s never really learned to actually retrieve anything - but that’s ok. She loves to just mosey along. To go out on the porch and just sniff the sweet evening air. She teaches us patience. She is a foodie - impressively drooling at the whiff of a good morsel. You can set your watch by her mealtimes, when she’ll start winking and wagging, just to remind you. If you wear black, you will leave with half a ton of white hair on your pants. We find clumps of her fur on the couch, on the kitchen floor, sometimes in a suitcase when we’re halfway around the world (How??).

She is getting pretty old. (I hear her.) It’s harder to get up the stairs, and sometimes we have to walk along side her to help her get up a flight. Sometimes it takes a while for her to stand up, yet the way she still greets you at the door - like you are the most amazing person in the whole world - makes you feel pretty special - with that whole-body wag of joy.

Oh, my sweet companion. I love you, girl.
